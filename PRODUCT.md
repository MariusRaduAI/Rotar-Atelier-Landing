# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Companies and event organisers in Bucharest (offices booking lunches and
meetings, agencies and production crews, people planning private events and
gifts), plus private clients. Some read Romanian, some English, so the page
ships both with a flag switch. They arrive comparing against generic office
catering, they are short on time, and they worry about one thing above all:
will there be enough, and will it arrive on time. They convert by phone,
WhatsApp, or the request form.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Rotar Atelier, a private-chef catering studio: home-made
food, never frozen, cooked live at the client's venue or delivered fresh.
There is a set menu, and anything outside it can be built to order. The page
exists to get a call, a WhatsApp message or a quote request, by making the
quality and the calm of the service obvious in one read.

Page order follows the client's own questions: What we cater, How we cater,
We come to you, Calm service, Gallery and social proof, Start (lead form).
The private chef, live-cooking idea is the lead differentiator and belongs
in the hero, not buried.

## Brand Personality

Precise, warm, unshowy. Confident enough to be plain: photography, hairline
rules, and a Didone serif carry the tone. Professional first. The drama lives
in the food and the type, not in effects.

## Anti-references

- Anything that reads as AI-generated: Cormorant + Jost pairing, giant
  outlined watermark type, scrolling tickers, decorative glyphs or emoji,
  gradient gold, glass cards, an eyebrow label above every heading, numbered
  scaffolding on things that are not a sequence, "Not X, it's Y" headlines.
- Invented proof. No fabricated testimonials, statistics, response-time
  promises or capacity limits. If a claim is not confirmed by the client, it
  does not ship (or it ships flagged for confirmation).
- Generic "premium bakery" templates: centred layouts, stock cream-and-gold
  everywhere, card grids.

## Design Principles

1. **Say only what is true.** Copy states what the client confirmed. Anything
   inferred is listed for review, never dressed up as fact.
2. **Photography and type do the work.** Real photos from the kitchen; no
   decoration that would exist without them.
3. **One idea per section, a different composition each.** Menu-style index,
   image beside principles, sequence, one flat gold statement, mosaic, form.
4. **Never make the visitor hunt for the way to order.** Call, WhatsApp and
   the form are one tap away at every scroll depth on mobile.
5. **Bilingual by design.** Every string lives in `i18n.js`; nothing is
   hard-coded in one language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, including small text on cream (gold text uses a darker ink
tone) and low-opacity text on black. Visible focus states, skip link, real
labels on every field, `aria-live` form status, `prefers-reduced-motion`
respected. Language switch exposes state with `aria-pressed` and sets
`<html lang>`.
