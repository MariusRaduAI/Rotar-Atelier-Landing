# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Corporate event organizers and companies in Bucharest sourcing artisanal
catering (platters, group brunch) for corporate events and team gatherings.
They land on the page to quickly judge whether the quality matches "premium"
before committing — then convert via phone call, WhatsApp, or the offer
form. Time-pressured, comparing against generic office catering, wary of
anything that looks like a template.

## Product Purpose

Rotar Atelier — The Experience is the landing page for an artisanal
bakery/catering studio (platters, group brunch, fine pastry) targeting
corporate events. The page exists to convert visitors into inbound
call/WhatsApp/form leads by making the artisanal difference (fresh-baked
daily, no frozen product, no margarine) undeniable at a glance — not just
claimed in copy. Success = a visitor believes this is a serious, considered
craft business within the first screen, and finds a fast path to contact.

## Brand Personality

Bold, theatrical, extravagant — but earned by real craft, not costume drama.
Confident use of scale, contrast, and asymmetry; dramatic imagery treatment;
willing to take a visual risk per section rather than repeating one safe
template. Not shy, not generic-minimalist-luxury, not centered-and-safe.

## Anti-references

The generic "premium bakery/hospitality" AI-template look — which is what
the current build has drifted into:
- A tiny uppercase tracked kicker above every single section (DESPRE ATELIER,
  CE OFERIM, CUM FUNCȚIONEAZĂ, PENTRU CINE LUCRĂM...) — the 2023-era AI tell.
- Numbered 01/02/03/04 scaffolding on services, craft points, and the
  timeline, used as decoration rather than because the content is a real
  sequence.
- Safe, centered, symmetric section layouts repeated top to bottom.
- Stock-feeling gold-gradient-on-black treatment with no texture or surprise.

## Design Principles

1. **Real craft over decoration.** Every dramatic visual choice is grounded
   in the actual product (photography, texture, materiality) — never
   gratuitous ornament for its own sake.
2. **One unmistakable move per section.** No uniform template stamped
   eight times. Each section earns its own composition, scale, and rhythm.
3. **Confidence over caution.** Big type scale, real contrast, asymmetric
   layout. Nothing defaults to centered-and-safe.
4. **Preserve, don't discard, the approved brand system.** The RT mark,
   black/cream/gold palette, and Cormorant Garamond + Jost pairing are
   client-approved and stay — but their expression should be pushed far
   harder than a typical brand deck would.
5. **Every flourish still serves the CTA.** Theatrical, not decorative —
   conversion (call / WhatsApp / offer form) stays the throughline under
   the drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA contrast throughout (body text ≥4.5:1, large/display text
≥3:1) despite the bold palette. Every animation ships a
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (crossfade or instant). No motion-only
affordances — states also communicated via color/position/text.
